# Consistency

Plans change as they sharpen, and a later decision can break what an earlier one relied on. Look for:

- Decisions or steps that contradict each other.
- Steps that rely on something a later decision removed or changed.
- Steps in an order that breaks a dependency, such as a step that uses what a later step creates.
- Goals the plan states that no step delivers.

Done when you have checked every step against each decision and step it depends on.
